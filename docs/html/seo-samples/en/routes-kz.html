What changes when you apply from Kazakhstan

Three things depend on the country you apply from: whether your school certificate or diploma is recognised for direct entry, whether you need a visa and which one, and how you prove that you can cover living costs. Each country page below describes these points for applicants from Kazakhstan.

How to compare countries

Compare countries by language of instruction, admission route, living costs, work rights during studies and the time needed to prepare. Do not choose by tuition alone: compare the total cost over the whole program.
